Frequently asked

What is easiYESt?

easiYESt is a free wedding planning app that brings together everything you need: a vendor directory, vendor comparison, budget tracking, guest list management, digital RSVPs, and a full wedding checklist — all in one place.

Is easiYESt really free?

Yes. Completely free for couples. No credit card required, no hidden fees, and no paywalled planning tools.

How is easiYESt different from The Knot?

The Knot displays vendors based on who pays for premium placement. easiYESt lists vendors without paid rankings, is ad-free, and combines all your planning tools into a single clean interface.

How is easiYESt different from Zola?

Zola is excellent for registries and wedding websites, but its vendor marketplace still relies on paid placements. easiYESt is focused entirely on vendor discovery and planning tools, fully free with no upgrade required.

Can I collect RSVPs with easiYESt?

Yes. easiYESt generates a unique RSVP link you can share via text, email, or social. Guests respond online and it syncs directly to your guest list — no paper RSVPs needed.

Does easiYESt work on mobile?

Yes. easiYESt works on all devices — desktop, tablet, and mobile. It's available as a web app and on iOS, so you can plan your wedding anywhere.

What types of vendors can I find?

Photographers, videographers, venues, caterers, florists, DJs, bands, officiants, hair and makeup artists, cake designers, and more. Save your favorites, compare them side by side, and add them to your budget with one tap.
